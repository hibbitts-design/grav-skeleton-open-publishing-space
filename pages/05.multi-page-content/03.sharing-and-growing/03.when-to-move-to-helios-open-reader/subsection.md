Open Publishing Space suits smaller amounts of content, especially alongside a blog. If your content grows into something more substantial – a standalone open textbook or course reader, or several readers on one site – [Helios Open Reader](https://github.com/hibbitts-design/grav-skeleton-helios-open-reader) is the better fit.

This guide uses the same page settings as Helios Open Reader (subtitle, authors, edition, license, section labels, learning objectives and section card details), and the same shortcodes and callouts, so your content can move across with little rework.
