Start each page with a sentence or two that tells readers what it's about, then break the rest into short parts with headings.

## A Heading

Headings start with `##`, so readers can scan the page and jump to a part. Lists are just as quick:

- Start a line with a dash for a bullet list
- Or with `1.` for numbered steps

Links look like this: [the Markdown Guide](https://www.markdownguide.org/).
