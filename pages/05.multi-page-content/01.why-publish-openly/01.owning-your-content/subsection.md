Everything on this site is stored as plain Markdown files. You can edit them in the Admin panel, sync them with a Git repository, and move them to another host or tool whenever you like.

[key-takeaways]
- Your content is plain text you control, not data locked inside a platform.
- Git Sync keeps a full history of every change.
[/key-takeaways]
