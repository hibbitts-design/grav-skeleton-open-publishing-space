Readers on the web tend to scan before they read. Short paragraphs, clear headings and the occasional list make a page easier to take in, on a phone as much as on a large screen.

[example]
Rather than one long page, this guide splits its content into units and pages – the progress bar below shows where you are, and the Previous/Next links take you through in order.
