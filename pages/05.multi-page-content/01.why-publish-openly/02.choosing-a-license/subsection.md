A [Creative Commons](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/) license tells readers what they may do with your work. **CC BY 4.0** is a common choice for open educational resources: anyone may reuse and adapt it, as long as they credit you.

> [!TIP]
> Set the license once on the reader's home page (**Attribution** tab) and the OER attribution appears on every page of the guide.
