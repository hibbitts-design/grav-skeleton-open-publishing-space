# Getting Started

Welcome to your Open Publishing Space. It comes with an example guide, Open Education Essentials, and one more that's switched off – a Sample Guide to start your own from.

**How editing works** – open a page in [**Pages**](admin:/pages), change its text on the **Content** tab and its settings on the other tabs, then **Save**. Text is written in Markdown, such as `**bold**`, `## Heading` and `- list item`.

**How the home page works** – **Guides** shows a card for each published guide; with just one, the logo goes straight to that guide. [View the home page](../../) to see it as visitors do.

## Start with Your Site

1. **Name** your site – set **Site Title** in [**Configuration → Site**](admin:/config/site)
2. **Add** your logo and colour – set **Custom Logo** and **Accent Color** in [**Themes → My Theme**](admin:/themes/mytheme)
3. **Change** the welcome text on the [**Guides**](admin:/pages/edit/guides) page

## Make a Guide Your Own

Start from the [**Sample Guide**](admin:/pages/edit/sample-guide), a small guide with writing prompts on every page – it's switched off, so turn on **Published** to see it. Or adapt [Open Education Essentials](admin:/pages/edit/open-education-essentials), a full example.

1. **Open** the guide in **Pages**
2. **Rename** it with **Guide Title**, and set its **Subtitle** and **Author(s)** on its **Details** tab
3. **Add** a cover – upload a picture under **Page Media**, then choose it as **Cover Image** on **Details**
4. **Pick** a **Guide Color**, also on **Details**, for a light tint behind the header on all its pages
5. **Edit** its sections and the pages inside them, which appear in folder order – **Add Page** creates new ones
6. **Set** its license and credit on its **Attribution** tab

For another guide, use **Copy page** on a guide you've set up.

## Remove What You Don't Need

- **An example guide** – delete it, or turn off **Published** to keep it as a reference
- **The site's [Standard Page](admin:/pages/edit/standardpage) or [Blog](admin:/pages/edit/blog)** – delete it, or turn on **Hide from Menu** on its **Advanced** tab
- **The switched-off examples** ([modular](admin:/pages/edit/modular-page) and [Multi-Section](admin:/pages/edit/multi-section-page) pages) – keep them as references, or delete them

For every setting and more how-tos, see the [Open Publishing Space README](https://github.com/hibbitts-design/grav-skeleton-open-publishing-space#readme) on GitHub.
